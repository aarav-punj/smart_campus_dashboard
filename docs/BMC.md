**Bennett University | 2026CSAE102 - Technology and Sustainability**

## Sustainability Outcomes & Impact Metrics

- **Mean Time to Detect (MTTD):** Targeting a reduction in average anomaly detection latency from several days to under 2 hours, enabling staff to halt overflows and pipe ruptures immediately.
- **Resource Conservation Metrics:** Tracking cumulative litres of water and kWh of electrical power saved per hostel block through the elimination of unmonitored nocturnal leaks and after-hours equipment loads.
- **Operational Resolution Rate:** Measuring the proportion of system alerts acknowledged and physically resolved by facilities personnel within 24 hours.
- **Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Alignment:** Primarily mapped to SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production) through active water conservation and leak mitigation, with SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation) through detecting nocturnal water leaks and overflows, and SDG 7 (Affordable and Clean Energy) by curbing avoidable institutional energy waste.
